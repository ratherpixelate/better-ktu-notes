KTU Exam Pattern & Evaluation Blueprint

University evaluators spend less than 90 seconds reviewing an answer booklet. Structuring your answer around the expected marking scheme guarantees maximum marks:

Part A · 3-Mark Questions

10 Compulsory questions (30 Marks total). State the exact definition in 2 lines, write 1 key technical keyword/formula, and cite 1 concrete example.

Part B · 14-Mark Essays

5 Module-wise choices (70 Marks total). Always draw the labeled block diagram first (fetches 4-5 marks), followed by 5 numbered points and worked steps.

Numericals & Trace Tables

In IEEE 754 conversions or stack traces, write down initial bit patterns, show every intermediate step, and clearly box the final output with units.
